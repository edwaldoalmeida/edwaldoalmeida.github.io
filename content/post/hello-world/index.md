## Why I finally started writing

I've been meaning to do this for a while.

My career looks like a zigzag on paper. Software engineering in healthcare. Ten years in infrastructure and networking before that. Certifications in Linux, CISCO and Project Management. A stretch running a real estate business, selling and managing properties along the way.

They all reward the same skill: finding what's quietly breaking before it breaks loudly, and fixing it so it stays fixed.

Right now that work has me deep in test automation — it's my specialty, and where a lot of the interesting problems are. But the engineering underneath it is what I actually write about.

So this is my little corner of the internet. Notes on software engineering, the systems I build, and occasionally the parts of the job that don't fit in a pull request.
